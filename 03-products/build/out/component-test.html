DRILL · THE FOOD TEST5 REPS
  1. Stand at your start distance. Wait for the dog to notice the trigger.
  2. Offer one pea-sized treat at the nose.
  3. Score the mouth: instant / slow / refused.
✓ PASS — MOVE ON WHEN
  • Dog eats instantly on 3 straight reps
  • Loose leash the whole time
✕ STOP — BACK UP WHEN
  • Dog refuses food twice
  • Any bark, lunge, or freeze
⚡ IF IT GOES WRONG

Dog explodes mid-rep? U-turn, add two cars of distance, breathe, restart at the easier line.
