# LAUNCH PLAN — CalmWalk Meta Ads

## Phase 1 — Validation (days 1–4)
- **One CBO campaign**, purchase optimization, broad targeting (no interest stacks — US, 25–65,
  all genders; the creative does the targeting), **$100–150/day**.
- **3 ad sets = 3 angles**, each running ONLY its LEAD ad:
  `a1-1-lead-5am-street` · `a2-1-lead-zonemap` · `a3-1-lead-quote-vs-book`.
- Pixel events: Purchase primary; ViewContent/InitiateCheckout as diagnostics only.
- **Kill rule:** any ad at 2× target CPA ($80) after $50 spend → off. No exceptions, no feelings.
- **72 hours hands-off** otherwise. No edits, no budget nudges — the learning phase eats edits.

## Phase 2 — Feed the winner (days 4–10)
- Identify the winning angle by CPA (tie-break: hook rate = 3-sec views / impressions).
- Add the winner's 6 workhorse siblings (formats 2–7) into the winning ad set, 2–3 at a time.
- Keep the two losing LEADs alive only if under 1.5× target CPA; otherwise off.
- Target CPA: **$40** (breakeven $62 — see offer map). Healthy launch = $35–50 blended.

## Phase 3 — Scale (day 10+)
- Scale CBO budget **+20% per day** while blended CPA ≤ target; pause scaling 48h after any breach.
- Wildcards (`a*-8-wild-*`) enter ONLY when winning-ad frequency > 2.5 — they are pattern
  breaks for fatigued audiences, not openers.
- Refresh cadence: 2 new variants of the winning format every 2 weeks (same template, new hooks
  from the research VOC bank).
- When testimonials exist (permissioned, real): unhide the sales-page slots and cut testimonial
  versions of the winning ad — expect the whole funnel's economics to step up; re-baseline then.

## Account hygiene
- Domain verified, pixel + CAPI before a dollar is spent (operator to-do).
- All ads run first-person/dog-framing; zero person-attribute callouts; zero outcome-timeline
  promises; zero fabricated social proof — this is both policy survival and brand.
- Comments: hide nothing except spam; answer "does this work for [breed]" with the honest
  fear/frustration answer from the FAQ. Comment replies are free conversion copy.
