**Scoring rubric** (meta-eval skill absent from repo; rubric declared here and applied identically to every ad):
- Thumb-stop hook strength (0–25): does the first glance create a question or recognition?
- Clarity at feed size (0–20): single message readable on a phone without zoom
- Native-ness (0–15): does it blend into the feed for its format class?
- Message–avatar match (0–20): speaks VOC language to the solution-aware reactive-dog owner
- Compliance (0–10): no person-attribute callouts, no outcome promises, no fabricated social proof
- Brand/offer coherence (0–10): palette, mechanism naming, page-number specificity
Threshold: **80**. Scores recorded after visual inspection of the generated files; any <80 → diagnose, regenerate (max 2), keep best.
